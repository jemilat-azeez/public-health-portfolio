# Project 3: HPV Vaccination Awareness Campaign

Health promotion posters I designed as a volunteer for a University of Derby
and Derbyshire County Council HPV awareness campaign for students.

## What it shows
- Plain-language HPV facts, myth-busting and a clear call to action
- Statistics referenced to NHS, WHO, Cancer Research UK and other sources
- QR code signposting to NHS information
